Shuttler

One phone clip of a badminton match in. Rally structure, tempo, a coaching cue and a running score out, with a plain statement of what one camera cannot call.

A contract-first pipeline: calibrate the court from painted lines, track the shuttle with TrackNet, track players with YOLO, segment rallies, classify outcomes, score, report, correct. Heavy models run out of process. The hosted version runs the rally-segmentation path on a vision-language model and needs no GPU.

What the evidence closed. Eight engineering probes, replicated at a second venue, converged on one fact: ordinary single-camera footage does not record the shuttle near contact. So landing, line calls and winner stay held, the report says so on every page, and the product became intelligence from commodity footage rather than a line judge.

Privacy is fail-closed by design: a blur-when-unsure minor classifier, an output guard that refuses an unblurred minor, and raw video that is never served to share-link viewers. Python, Cloud Run, Google sign-in with an allowlist. The repository is private.

Barony

Git-native governance for teams of AI coding agents: who may do what, who did what, what is true now.

Run several coding agents on one long-lived project for a few weeks and the same walls appear: state chaos, rules that decay into theater, knowledge rot, and an accountability vacuum. Barony is a spec, a CLI called baron, and per-runtime adapters. Each persona is declared once and hydrated onto Claude Code, code-puppy or a pydantic-ai harness. The substrate is plain markdown and git. No server, no database.

The idea it turns on: an instruction is not a control. baron guard blocks denied actions before the tool runs, and every capability is labelled honestly as enforced or merely instructed. The tool refuses to round up.

enCodr

Compile ideas into visual communication.

A presentation treated as a product: structured source, reviewable decisions, repeatable builds, many delivery formats. Human intent becomes a narrative model of claims, evidence and the decision the audience should reach. That compiles to a semantic intermediate representation, then through a visual compiler and scene graph to PPTX, web, PDF or video.

The same reviewed source yields a ten-minute leadership briefing and a forty-five-minute engineering talk. Shortening preserves qualifications and dependencies instead of deleting slides. PPTX is an output and an escape hatch; the semantic source stays authoritative.

Canonical thesis, design principles, architecture and nine ratified architecture decisions, plus a TypeScript prototype with a gate that runs dependency boundaries, typecheck, lint and the full suite on PostgreSQL and Chromium. The repository is private.
